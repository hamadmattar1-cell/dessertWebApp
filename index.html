Apple Bavarian Torte (★ ★ ★ ★)


A classic eropean torte backed in a springform pan. Cream cheese, sliced almonds, and apples make this the perfect holiday treat(12 servings).

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup all purpose flour
  • 1 package cream cheese
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

Directions

  1. preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Cream together butter, sugar, vanilla and flour
  3. press crust mixture into the flat bottom of a 9 inch springorm pan. set aside
  4. In a medium bowl, beat together cream cheese, sugar, egg and vanilla until smooth. Pour over crust
